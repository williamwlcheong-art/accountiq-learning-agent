 Introduction
 This indicative valuation has been prepared to provide the owner with an informed view of the fair market value of 100% of
 the operating business. It draws on the financial information supplied, owner-confirmed private facts and independently
 researched public market evidence.

 Valuation Methodology Adopted
 Discounted cash flow is the primary valuation method because it directly reflects the company's maintainable future
 cash-generating capacity. Researched EV/EBITDA evidence provides an independent reasonableness cross-check.




AccountIQ                                                                                                              10
 ACCOUNTIQ INDICATIVE VALUATION



 Valuation Approach and Assumptions
 The model uses a five-year explicit forecast period, 8.0% annual growth during the forecast period, a 2.5% terminal growth
 rate anchored to researched New Zealand inflation and the 28% New Zealand corporate tax rate.

 Multiples Cross-check

 Sensitivity and Specific Risks
 The valuation is most sensitive to owner transition, retention of larger customers, renewal of recurring contracts and delivery
 of the stated forward pipeline. These factors should be revisited if circumstances change before a transaction.
