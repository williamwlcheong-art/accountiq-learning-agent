## Email

Write to **hello@accountiq.co.nz**. That address reaches a person, not a queue,
and you can expect a reply within one working day.

## If you already have a report

Reply to any email AccountIQ has sent you and it lands in the same place, with
your report already attached to the conversation. That is the quickest route if
your question is about a specific valuation.

## Password and account problems

During early access, passwords are reset by hand. Email the address above from
the account you signed up with and it will be sorted the same day.

## Accountants and advisors

If you are looking at AccountIQ for your clients rather than your own business,
the [advisors page](/for-advisors) covers how that works. Email is still the
best way to start.
