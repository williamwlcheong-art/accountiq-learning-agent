## Who is responsible

**[Legal entity name, registered address]** operates AccountIQ and is
responsible for the personal information described here.

## What we collect

**Your account details.** Your email address and a secure form of your password.
We never store the password itself.

**What you upload.** The financial statements and business information you give
us, and the answers you provide during a valuation.

**Payment records.** The amount, the date and the status of a payment, along
with an identifier from our payment provider. Card numbers go directly to the
payment provider and never reach us.

**Basic technical information.** Standard web server records such as the pages
requested and the time of the request.

## Why we collect it

To create and secure your account, to prepare and review your valuation report,
to take payment, to email you when a report is ready, and to answer you when you
get in touch.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your financial statements to
advertise to you.

## Who else sees it

**Our payment provider, Stripe,** handles payments and receives your email
address and the amount charged.

**Our email provider** delivers the messages we send you.

**Our AI provider, Anthropic,** processes the text of your uploaded statements
to extract the figures and draft the report. That processing happens only to
prepare your report, and the content is not used to train their models.

**A reviewer at AccountIQ** reads your draft report, and the statements behind
it, before it is released.

Beyond that we disclose personal information only where the law requires it.

## Where it is stored

Your information is held on servers operated by our hosting provider. Some of
the providers above operate overseas, which means your information may be stored
or processed outside New Zealand. We use providers that are required to protect
it to a standard comparable to the Privacy Act 2020.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and your reports while your account is open, so you can
come back to a report later.

If you close your account, we delete your uploaded statements and reports within
**[retention period to confirm]** and keep the minimum payment records that tax
and company law require us to keep.

## Keeping it safe

Access to customer information is limited to the people who need it to run the
service. Passwords are stored in a hashed form, connections use encryption in
transit, and sessions are protected by signed cookies.

## Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold
about you, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Email
**hello@accountiq.co.nz** and we will respond within 20 working days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of
the Privacy Commissioner at **privacy.org.nz**.

## Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for advertising or
tracking across other websites.
