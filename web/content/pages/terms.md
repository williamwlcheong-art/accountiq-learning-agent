A report is indicative and is intended to support your own decision making.

A report is not financial advice, investment advice, accounting advice, tax
advice or legal advice. It is not a certified, official or court-standard
valuation, and it is not prepared by a registered valuer. It is not suitable for
use in court proceedings, tax filings or formal disputes, and it does not meet
any professional valuation standard.

You are responsible for deciding whether to act on a report, and for obtaining
professional advice before you do.

You must have the right to supply the financial statements and other information

A valuation reflects the information it is given. If the information is wrong or
incomplete, the report will be too, and that remains your responsibility.
