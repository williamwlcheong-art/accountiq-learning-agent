## One report, one fee

A valuation report costs **$FEE including GST**. That is the whole cost. There
is no subscription, no per-hour billing and no charge for the account itself.

You see the fee on screen before you pay, after you have uploaded your
statements and answered the questions. Nothing is charged before that point, so
you can stop at any time up to it and owe nothing.

## What the fee covers

- Reading your financial statements and pulling out the figures
- The valuation itself, including the assumptions you confirm
- A written report covering the business, its financial performance, the
  methodology, the valuation range and the risks
- A reviewer checking the report before it is released to you
- The report online and as a PDF, kept in your account

## What it does not cover

The report is indicative. It is not financial advice, and it is not a certified,
official or court-standard valuation. It will not satisfy a court, the Inland
Revenue Department or a formal dispute process, and it is not a substitute for a
registered valuer where one is required.

It also does not include an advisor reading it with you. If you want that
conversation, email us and we will tell you what it involves.

## Refunds

If something goes wrong on our side and no report is released to you, the
payment is refunded in full.

Once a reviewed report has been released to your account, the work is done and
the fee is not refundable. If you think the report has a genuine error, reply to
your confirmation email and it will be looked at properly.

## Payment

Payment runs through Stripe. Card details go to Stripe and never reach
AccountIQ.

Prices are in New Zealand dollars. Australian support is planned and not
available yet.
