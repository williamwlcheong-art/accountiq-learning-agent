## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Category-interchangeable app on top of a landing page that has started to find a voice. Product character shows in the honesty copy, the hero report-preview card, and the readiness card naming the customer's own statements by period. It stops at the wizard door: the intake reads like a generic form builder, and globals.css is three design systems stacked by append (four :root blocks, three blues, two font stacks). The portal refactor unified the shell by aliasing the old tokens rather than deleting them.

**Deterministic scan:** two static findings, both pre-existing and in globals.css: `border-accent-on-rounded` on the landing hero preview (8px border-top) and `overused-font` (Arial, dead rule, now removed). Runtime detector: `flat-type-hierarchy` on login, register, wizard and account (1.5:1 to 1.9:1), `line-length` on the readiness card, plus the landing findings already recorded on 2026-09-01. No contrast failures, no missing labels, no console errors. Touch targets under 44px were confined to landing nav links and the old app nav.

**Browser evidence:** overlays could not be shown because the Browser pane was hidden for most of the run; findings come from console capture and DOM measurement.

## Overall Impression

Honest, legally careful, and technically thorough in its failure states, but the customer was dropped into step one of a form on sign-in, wore three brands across one session, could lose ten minutes of intake to a refresh, and at the end got a bare "Internal Server Error" instead of the PDF they paid for. The shell and delivery problems are fixed; the intake form is the remaining weight.

## What's Working

1. Honesty as brand: "Indicative only. Not financial advice." under the hero CTA, in the review section, and in every signed-in footer; the FAQ answers "Is this financial advice?" with "No".
2. Failure states are designed, not defaulted: clarification card names the statement, period and reason and says no payment was taken; the status card distinguishes seven end states.
3. The confirmation card binds what is being bought: fee, coverage, every assumption with provenance, and the frozen-inputs notice.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P0, fixed] Download PDF returned a raw 500.** Backend now returns a readable 503; the button fetches, handles failure inline, and offers the online report. Root cause locally was WeasyPrint missing Pango (installed).
2. **[P0, fixed] Report viewer back links left the app.** Pointed at the backend origin and a fresh wizard; now go to /reports (customer) and /admin/reports (admin).
3. **[P1, fixed] Wizard state lost on refresh or Back.** Progress from readiness to confirmation now resumes from sessionStorage; the intake step warns before unload.
4. **[P1] Intake form is one 40-control screen.** Split into Assumptions, Adjustments, and Risk ratings using the new step bar; render the rationale textareas only when relevant; show the extracted figures beside the questions. Suggested: /impeccable distill.
5. **[P1] Report type picker shows four products that cannot be bought.** Readiness already names the product and fee; drop the step and mention the pilot products on /reports. Product decision for William. Suggested: /impeccable distill.
6. **[P2] Three stacked design systems in globals.css.** Collapse to one :root with the marketing tokens and delete the legacy shell block. Suggested: /impeccable extract.
7. **[P2, fixed] Dates on the UTC calendar day, profile menu focus, warning-pill contrast, mobile stepper without numbers, float artefact in the depreciation field.**

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** "Terminal growth rate" with no help (now hinted); capex panel says it cannot derive a figure then asks for one; ratings where 2, 3, 4 have no label; four "Coming later" cards right after the readiness high point.

**Sam (keyboard, screen reader):** drop-zone focus ring was invisible (fixed); stepper announces labels but no "step 2 of 4"; four disabled picker buttons skipped with no explanation; the intake heading outline is thin for 60 fields.

**Casey (one-handed mobile):** old app nav became a horizontal scroller (fixed: two-row header); rating grid stays five across at 375px; "Start another valuation" was a thumb-height reset beside the primary (now secondary, with "Back to your valuations" primary).

**Riley (stress tester):** renaming a .txt to .pdf passes extension-only validation until readiness fails; a stale localStorage report id after a database swap shows a status card that never resolves; admin Approve has no confirmation before release.

## Minor Observations

- "Built for New Zealand and Australian SMEs" while every currency and date is en-NZ; no AUD anywhere.
- Landing steps: seven items in a four-column grid leave an orphan slot.
- Two check-mark styles (CSS glyph on landing, inline SVG in the portal empty state).
- Product is called "a Business Valuation", "Valuation Advisory", and "the valuation advisory" in three places.
- No turnaround promise anywhere for the review wait.
- Report viewer meta shows a raw timestamp; no valuation headline near the top; no print or download control inside the viewer.
- No currency code or GST wording next to $495.00 (pricing copy is William's).

## Questions to Consider

1. If the reviewer checks every draft anyway, should the customer supply defaults they can override rather than blanks they must fill?
2. What does the report type picker exist for, other than to advertise things that cannot be bought?
3. The arc ends on "We will email you". What would it take to put a date on that sentence?
